How the decision is made

Every calculation is deterministic. AI explains the evidence after the numbers are computed.

01

Normalize the order

Compare equivalent SKUs, units and delivery scope. The order quantity is the larger of demand and MOQ, rounded up to the supplier's pack size.

Order = ceil(max(demand, MOQ) / pack) × pack
02

Calculate landed cost

Convert goods and incremental shipment costs to THB. Add modeled duty and non-recoverable VAT; show cash outlay separately.

Duty base = goods + freight + insurance
VAT base = duty base + duty
Cost = duty base + duty + other + non-recoverable VAT
03

Check feasibility first

Exclude expired quotes, insufficient capacity, late delivery, low quality or low OTIF. An ineligible supplier cannot win on price.

When none qualify, the app recommends no award.

04

Rank eligible offers

Cost score = lowest eligible cost / supplier cost × 100. Lead score = fastest eligible lead time / supplier lead time × 100. Quality and OTIF use their input percentages.

Score = Σ(component × weight / 100)

Ties use unrounded score, then lower cost, then quote ID. Changing the comparison set can change the relative scores.

Model boundaries

AI mode

The default brief is a rule-based explanation, not a language model. Optional OpenAI explanations use computed evidence, cannot change the ranking, and need your explicit consent to send the analysis. Model output still needs human review. No purchase orders are created.
